Awakened Fit is a real-world fitness application with RPG-inspired gamification.

Its purpose is to help users improve their real-world fitness through workouts, fitness tracking, personalized programs, challenges, and progression.

The concept is:

"Train in real life. Grow as a hunter."

IMPORTANT:
Awakened Fit is NOT the RPG game.

The future game will be a completely separate project. I will decide how to develop that game later.

Do not build:
- Unity integration.
- Game synchronization.
- Game authentication.

- Game-specific APIs.

Keep the fitness app self-contained.

The app can have hunter levels, ranks, attributes, quests, and achievements as motivational gamification features. These features must relate to real-world fitness activities.
